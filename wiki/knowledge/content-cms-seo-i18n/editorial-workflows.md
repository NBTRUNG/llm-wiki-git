# Editorial Workflows

## Checks

- Roles and permissions are named.
- Draft, preview, review, publish, unpublish, archive, and rollback states are
  defined where relevant.
- Preview does not leak unpublished/private content.
- Audit trail exists for sensitive or public-facing changes.
- Broken links, missing media, expired content, and scheduled publication are
  tested when supported.
