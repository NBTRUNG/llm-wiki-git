## Intent

Keep LLM-Wiki focused on durable knowledge for agents and humans:

- read the right files, not every file;
- keep source of truth in Markdown files that can be reviewed and diffed;
- coordinate multi-agent work without a database or derived context cache;
- preserve retired Hermes material only as historical evidence.
- keep each directory's purpose discoverable without making every basename
  `index.md`;
- make knowledge-pack usage measurable through result reports.
